How does peer pressure online impact the decisions we make?

What it Contains

18 presentation slides covering digital belonging, social media psychology, and 4 teenage case studies.

Target Audience

Middle and high school students, educators, digital wellness counselors, and parents.

Core Purpose

To help young people recognize unconscious online compliance and cultivate empathy without pressure.

Core Concept A

Belonging

The human need and feeling of being accepted, valued, and included within a group or community.

Core Concept B

Peer Pressure

The influence from others that pushes someone to adopt certain behaviors, attitudes, or values to fit in.

Lesson Learning Objectives

1

Understand the connection between the need for belonging and peer pressure online.

2

Identify digital peer pressure across viral trends, speedruns, and group chats.

3

Reflect on small actions to help peers feel valued for who they are both online and offline.
